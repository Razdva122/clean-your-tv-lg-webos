# Black Hole artwork

Video author: **Xecutioner**. Original: [4K SCI-FI Black Hole](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2981249186).

The maintainer confirmed the author's permission on 2026-11-04 for non-commercial use and inclusion in free CYTV distributions with explicit attribution to Xecutioner. The video and derived stills are not MIT artwork. See LICENSE.txt and CREDITS.txt. Private correspondence is not published. Commercial use needs separate permission.

The original Scene was downloaded through the owner's Steam installation and rendered in a 3840x2160 client area. OBS Game Capture used an equal 3840x2160 canvas/output at 30 fps, scale 1 and no crop. The monitor resolution did not determine the capture size. Scene clocks and random explosions were disabled in that capture window. No asset was spatially upscaled.

The retained master is 45 seconds. The packaged silent loop is 12 seconds at 30 fps; a one-second dissolve joins its original motion. UHD and FHD MP4s are validated separately. The streaming copy is remuxed, without re-encoding, into fragmented MP4 for a continuous MSE timeline. The source and streaming container have identical H264 elementary-stream bytes.

The former procedural blue-nebula artwork is retained in Git history. It is not the current Ribbon wallpaper.
