# Landing design review — 2026-11-10

GATE: PASS. No open FAIL; three applied waivers, 14 non-blocking warnings. This is website evidence, not a TV acceptance or publication record.

## Design and material

Kind: software. Clean Your TV gives LG webOS owners a configurable home screen.
The main action opens installation instructions; the secondary action opens the
existing standalone browser demo. All 13 website languages remain available.

References: [Taste Skill](https://www.tasteskill.dev/) and
[PlxNative](https://plxnative.com/). Borrowed ideas are large category-first type,
a prominent real product view, click-selected looks and a dark/light page rhythm.
Their code, copy, images and signature effects were not copied.

The brand palette retains orange on graphite: background #111213, surface
#1a1c1e, ink #f4f3ef, accent #ff5a30. The randomly offered teal palette was
replaced by the client's existing brand colours. Manrope was the next dealt
pairing after Reddit Sans, chosen for Cyrillic coverage; two local WOFF2 files
are 60 KiB combined. Languages outside its coverage use their platform fallback.
The centred hero replaces the initial triptych deal because this product needs
one coherent TV interface, not a collage of unrelated outputs. The selected
`topglow` ground is static. No shader or big scene was added: the captured product
navigation is the moving visual. The dealt `letters-assemble` moment runs only
in the apps section; Hindi uses `word-highlight` to preserve text shaping.
Unmodified Taste Skill 2.5.1 motion/moment assets and their MIT licence are
vendored in `website/vendor/taste/`.

Voice: factual and technical, addressed to the TV owner, with concrete actions
and explicit installation limits. No invented testimonials, adoption counts,
compatibility claims, prices, names or other facts. No stock photographs,
generated hardware, client-material stand-ins or missing asset slots. Real app
logos remain in the project's public simulated renderer captures. Canonical
URLs, article schema and the existing deployment domain remain configured.

Classic, Liquid Glass and Ribbon captures come from the bundled browser renderer
and its public demo inventory, not a personal TV. They are animated WebP, with
960×540 and 480×270 variants and matching stills. The landing loads no iframe or
TV runtime. Pause, reduced motion, no-script selection and failed-animation
fallback are covered. The real interactive renderer remains on `/demo/`.

## Gate exceptions and retained warnings

Applied waivers:

1. `no-visual`, editions: this is a factual specification comparison. A picture
   would not add installation or compatibility evidence.
2. `no-visual`, guides: editorial navigation needs accurate article titles and
   summaries; another screenshot would repeat the product without helping selection.
3. `copy-headings`, No-root/Root and Russian article headings: the actual edition
   names and localized nouns are meaningful; the English heuristic misses them.

The unused centred-frame waiver from an earlier iteration matched no final
finding and was not applied. There are no waived functional failures.

The retained warnings concern the hero image's share of its screen, secondary
14–15px summary text, spacing around full-screen sections and the close, mixed
navigation/control label sizes, the arrow's share of button space, longer
editorial summaries, the localized skip-link heuristic and the allowed
nav-plus-hero repetition of the installation action. These retain the actual
content and hierarchy; the skip link is the first keyboard target and points to
main content. There are no copy-lint warnings kept.

Performance's four warning observations concern vector logo sizing
and a below-fold lazy Classic capture classified as above-fold, each repeated at
the two widths. SVG logos remain vector assets; the second section's capture
stays lazy. None is a broken request or layout failure.

The first URL-based gate reused a stale URL snapshot; final acceptance was run
against actual exported HTML with an explicit root. No checker or kit source was
modified, and no page content was hidden from the checker. Long installation and
article text remains on the existing localized pages; phone article links omit
secondary summaries while keeping their titles and destinations.

Native-speaker editorial acceptance, physical TV behaviour, production delivery,
search ranking, LLM citations and conversion improvements are not established by
these website checks.
