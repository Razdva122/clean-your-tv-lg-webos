Clean Your TV offers a home screen without sponsored rows inside its own launcher. It is useful if you want a simpler place to open your LG webOS apps. It does not block ads throughout the television: LG Home, streaming apps, live channels and external devices have their own content and advertising.

## Where do you see the advertising?

Start by identifying the screen. A promoted tile on LG Home, an advertisement during a streaming video and a banner from an HDMI device come from different places. Changing the launcher affects the app-selection screen; it does not filter video playback or rewrite another application's interface.

If the promotion appears on LG Home, review the personalization and promotion settings available on your exact firmware. LG's menus vary between models, regions and updates, so a setting name found in an old guide may not exist on your TV. Use LG's model-specific manual rather than applying a service-menu procedure from a different television.

## What does Clean Your TV change?

The launcher gives you app ordering, a customizable dock and themes. Its screen contains no sponsored recommendation rows. Where Home routing is supported and enabled, the Home button can open this interface. LG Home remains available, so opening it can still show LG's own content.

You can [preview the interface](/clean-your-tv-lg-webos/demo/) in your browser. The preview shows layouts, not actual ad blocking or TV hardware behavior. See [how to change the home screen](/clean-your-tv-lg-webos/en/articles/change-lg-webos-home-screen/) for the setup choices.

## What stays outside the launcher's scope?

- Ads inside YouTube and other streaming apps are controlled by those services. The launcher does not provide a subscription or modify their playback.
- Recommendations and promotions on other LG screens remain outside its interface.
- Content from broadcast channels, set-top boxes and HDMI devices is not filtered.

Network-wide blocking is a separate approach with different trade-offs. Clean Your TV does not require you to replace your router's DNS settings. Avoid assuming that blocking a domain removes only ads; shared endpoints can also serve app functions.

## Choose based on the result you want

For a quieter app-selection screen, compare the themes and read the [Root and No-root comparison](/clean-your-tv-lg-webos/en/articles/root-vs-no-root/). Check [compatibility](/clean-your-tv-lg-webos/en/compatibility/) before installation; neither a model name nor a successful install proves reliable Home routing. For advertising during video playback, use the streaming service's own available options.

Keep recovery instructions nearby. You can return to LG Home, and the [help guide](/clean-your-tv-lg-webos/en/help/) explains Home control and interrupted setup. There is no need to change firmware just to compare the browser layouts.
