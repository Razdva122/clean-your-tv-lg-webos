Theme, scenes and wallpapers: aXistem (https://github.com/axistem-dev).

All wallpaper artwork is original. The three scenes are HTML/SVG/canvas drawings in
tools/scenes/, generated from code with a fixed random seed: layered gradients,
SVG fractal-noise clouds, procedural palm silhouettes, invented art-deco
facades with text-free neon shapes (a flamingo, a palm, a sun over waves),
wet-street and water reflections. tools/render-scenes.cjs renders them in
headless Chrome; tools/generate-wallpapers.py (Pillow) adds a fine film grain,
encodes the JPEGs. No photographs, stock images, AI image
models, screenshots, fonts, logos or third-party artwork were used.
