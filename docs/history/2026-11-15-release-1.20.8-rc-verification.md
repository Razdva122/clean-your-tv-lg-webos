## RC-02 published Home and candidate Helper on C3

The maintainer authorized the pre-dispatch probe on the project LG OLED55C3RLA.
The test used macOS ARM64 source cytv 0.20.8, No-root over the saved Root SSH
connection, and the saved network remote (physical remote model unknown).
The C3 started with Home 1.16.1, Helper 0.8.5, no Root edition packages and
both installer receipts idle.

- The ordinary installer installed Home 1.18.6 FHD with exact Helper 0.8.6
  and left both receipts idle. Readback matched Home metadata and both
  Helper state-permissions module hashes. From Media Player, one network HOME
  went through LG Home and reached Clean Your TV, which held at the one-second
  readback.
- Ordinary installer Restore kept both packages and stopped interception.
  One network HOME from Media Player reached LG Home and held at the
  one-second readback.
- Ordinary installer rollback restored published Helper 0.8.5 alongside
  Home 1.18.6. Both receipt and installed-file checks passed. One network
  HOME from Media Player again reached LG Home and held at the one-second
  readback. Both installer receipts were idle.

`verify_helper_transition` passed on the exact approved Helper archive
and published rollback packages. RC-02 passes all three selected subchecks.
Physical Magic Remote, standby, AC cold boot, audio and OTA were outside
this probe. RC-04 through RC-06 need final artifacts. RC-07 requires the
reporter device or matching state.
