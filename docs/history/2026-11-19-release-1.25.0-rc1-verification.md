## Exit reconciliation

Of seven originally selected C3 cases, seven passed on the tested configuration.
Observed product failures, cases satisfied only by reused evidence, concretely
blocked cases, deferrals among the selected C3 cases and independent untested
cases were all zero. The earlier Helper compatibility runs and model-specific
offline regressions are supporting evidence. The CI cancellation and the
test harness readback and readiness failures were recovered without product
changes or a second candidate build.

This pass covered the C3 system-profile Root backend and the ordinary
No-root path. Physical Magic Remote input and descriptor recreation, native
C1/G3 and webOS 5 routing, sound, AC power cycling and on-device signed OTA
were outside the C3 cases. Reported model-specific confirmation remains open
for the post-publication owner checks agreed in the release PR.
