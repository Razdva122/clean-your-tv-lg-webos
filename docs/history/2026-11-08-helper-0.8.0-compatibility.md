## Actual tests and outcomes

1. Ordinary Root full cleanup reached restart-required; one requested software
   reboot and same-goal continuation reached verified-clean. Saved trust and
   the original profile/theme backup were retained privately. Root edition was
   absent during the Helper physical test; previous Root observations were not
   reused as Helper evidence.
2. Ordinary No-root installation completed package/public-file/configuration
   readback with no pending operation. All 203 installed Home/Helper public files
   matched the prepared packages, with ordinary inventory personalization only.
3. Helper was stopped using the installer, then started through the published
   Home's actual Start Helper button and its confirmation. Same-package Home
   control status returned success/enabled; the actual controller's four UIDs
   were 5431, and the process probe reported running.
4. The owner opened an ordinary app and pressed short Home: our launcher opened.
   Readback independently confirmed Home foreground and Helper running.
5. The owner pressed triple Home: LG Home opened. A later short Home stayed on
   LG Home; arrows and volume buttons worked. Readback independently confirmed
   LG foreground, stopped Helper and no pending operation. Stop was preserved.

Expected and actual Home/escape behavior agree for this probe. No direct
controller launch, role/permission/credential/receipt edits, synthetic physical
Home observations or emergency-marker clearing were used.

A QA-only attempt to close the helper panel expected a different Back-button
label and refused before clicking it. Start Helper had already succeeded; a
separate process/UID probe confirmed running, and the owner used Back normally.
This harness mismatch is not a product failure and did not replace any physical
Home observation.

The earlier staging, fresh capture, System registration and removal findings,
fixes, retries and outcomes remain in the
[complete preliminary case/failure inventory](2026-11-08-c3-preliminary-qa.md).

## Scope and remaining evidence

Automatic No-root OTA/rollback, fresh stock Developer Mode provisioning, video
sound, standby/cold boot, other remotes, final candidate packages and universal
firmware compatibility were not verified by this probe. Volume-button success
is not video-audio acceptance. The Xiaomi Mi Box remote remains untested.

`verify_helper_transition` passed against the real previous packages, exact
candidate Helper and committed approval. Version/README checks passed; Homebrew
promotion is unselected. The full candidate must be built from a clean committed
source containing this approval, then pinned and accepted separately.
