## T17.1-01 Helper compatibility

2026-11-11 maintainer QA on OLED55C3RLA, firmware 43.21.71, webOS SDK 11.2.0, TV Node 20.12.2, LG Magic Remote (exact model unknown), macOS 15.6.1 ARM64, source cytv 0.17.2. Published Home 1.15.1 and prior Helper 0.8.1 were authenticated against the production-key-verified v1.17.0 feeds. Exact Helper 0.8.2 probe IPK SHA256 0740506104d526ee3334a03de65f0cc816095de27ba3b9ec0198b51a2b487781. With owner authorization, Root 0.16.1 was removed through the ordinary installer after capturing the current profile and verifying a private recovery backup. The same removal completed after the owner power-cycled the TV. Ordinary No-root installation through saved Root SSH verified 234 public files and left no pending operation. After ordinary installer Stop, the owner used Start Helper in published Home 1.15.1. Short Home from an app or input opened Clean Your TV. After remote-button standby and wake, the same boot identity, short Home routing, active controller UID 5431 and key mode 0640 were verified. Triple Home opened LG Home; another short Home stayed there, and arrows and volume worked. Readback confirmed Helper stopped, explicit Stop retained and no pending operation. The startup service reports running=false even during active controls; the observed process and Home routing establish the scoped result. Active cold boot is outside this rollback-compatibility probe.
