## Identity and coverage

The likely project meant by “glassnode что ли” is **Glasshouse**, https://github.com/rorygallagher2024/glasshouse. Official GitHub description: rooted LG webOS dashboard, privacy controls, debloating, ad/telemetry blocking and custom screensavers, plus optional MQTT. This is the repository previously called `lg-webos-dashboard`; early release changelog links also use `lg-webos-mqtt`. Treat the identity as a strongly supported match, rather than a certainty about the user's remembered name.

## Comparative implications (inferences, not measured rankings)

Glasshouse's strength is a broad rooted-TV operating console: telemetry, OLED care, privacy, debloat, app management, MQTT/Prometheus, diagnostics and recovery. It is much broader than merely a launcher. Custom Home itself only arrived v0.107.0 (Oct 17), roughly five weeks after v0.1.0. The very fast 205-release history contains repeated firmware-shape fixes, silent-success corrections and reversals of risky service/SAM assumptions. This is evidence to borrow test cases and capability boundaries, not a fair basis for a universal quality score.

For Clean Your TV: compare separate axes for no-root reach, root-required customization, published Home takeover support per webOS, safe escape/recovery, key semantics, standby versus cold boot, true privacy effect versus status readings, and original issue acceptance. Glasshouse already demonstrates long-Home fallback, heartbeat failback, partial startup survival, old-updater compatibility tests, HA schema checks, and settings backups; avoid claiming these absent. Its open evidence still leaves concrete opportunities around older-TV Home/input coverage, clear startup semantics, and preserving stock behavior around remaps/reboots.
