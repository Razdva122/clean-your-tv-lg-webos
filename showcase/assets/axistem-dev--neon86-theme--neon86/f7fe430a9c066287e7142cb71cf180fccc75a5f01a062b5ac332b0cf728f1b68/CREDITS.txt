Neon '86 credits

All wallpaper artwork is original. tools/generate-wallpapers.py renders it from code only
(Pillow and numpy, seed 1986): gradients, a striped sun, a perspective neon
grid, mountain and palm silhouettes, an abstract art-deco skyline with text-free
neon shapes, a rippled water reflection, haze, vignette and grain. No
photographs, stock images, AI image models, fonts, logos or third-party artwork
were used.

  Sunset       default wallpaper, 3840x2160: striped sun over a neon grid with palms
  Ocean Drive  extra wallpaper, 1920x1080: night art-deco skyline, neon, palms,
               ocean reflection (downscaled from a 3840x2160 render)

The preview (assets/sunset/preview.jpg, 960x540) is a screenshot of the Clean Your
TV browser preview with this theme and the Sunset wallpaper. It shows that
preview's demo app icons, which remain the property of their publishers and are
not covered by this theme's license (see preview/reference-apps/NOTICE.txt in
Clean Your TV).

Palette: hot pink #ff2e88, electric cyan #28dcff, violet #8a3cff, sunset
orange #ff9a45, sun yellow #ffe066, near-black navy #070818, ice text #e9f7ff.
